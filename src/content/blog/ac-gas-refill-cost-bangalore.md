**AC gas refill in Bangalore costs ₹1,800 – ₹3,500 in 2026 for most homes**, depending on tonnage (1 / 1.5 / 2 ton), refrigerant type (R32, R410A, or the older R22), and whether the leak was fixed before recharge. ## What "AC gas refill cost" actually includes

A proper refill visit isn't just decanting a cylinder into your unit. A real quote covers:

- **Leak detection** — UV dye injected earlier, or electronic sniffer scan around every joint
- **Leak repair** — usually a flare joint re-done, or a copper pinhole patched and pressure-tested
- **Vacuum test** — pumping the system down to remove all air and moisture before recharge
- **Charging to spec** — filling to the manufacturer's exact refrigerant weight (in grams), not "till the pressure feels right"
- **Cooling test** — running the unit for 20-30 minutes to confirm temperature delta and no re-leak

If a technician skips the vacuum test — or charges by pressure gauge alone rather than weight — the refill will be off-spec and cooling will be weak or the compressor will short-cycle. Ask before agreeing.

## R32 vs R410A vs R22 — which do you have?

- **R32** — most ACs installed in Bangalore from 2020 onwards. Slightly cheaper to refill, more energy-efficient, lower environmental impact. Check your indoor unit's label.
- **R410A** — the standard from roughly 2013–2019. Still widely serviced. Slightly more expensive per kg than R32.
- **R22** — being phased out globally. If your AC uses R22, refill is still possible but the gas itself is getting scarcer and more expensive year-over-year. Ask your technician whether a full unit replacement is worth pricing against continued R22 servicing.

## Red flags in a gas-refill quote

**"Just top it up — the leak is nothing."** Refrigerant doesn't leak "nothing." If it's low, there's a leak; if the leak isn't fixed, you'll be back to weak cooling within 2 to 6 months.

**A pressure gauge reading instead of a weight.** The right amount of refrigerant is specified on your AC's nameplate in grams. Pressure readings only tell you the current state, not how much to add.

**No vacuum test before charging.** Skipping this traps air and moisture inside the sealed loop, which corrodes the copper from the inside over months and eventually kills the compressor.

**Cash-only, no receipt.** A legitimate refill service issues an invoice and lists the refrigerant type, quantity in grams, and warranty on the work (typically 30 to 90 days on the recharge, plus manufacturer's part warranty on any replaced components).

## FAQs

**How long does an AC gas refill take?** A gas refill alone (with leak already located) takes 45 – 90 minutes. Add 30 – 60 minutes for leak detection and 30 – 90 minutes for the repair itself, depending on where the leak is.

**How long should a proper refill last?** With the leak actually fixed and the unit charged to spec, refrigerant in a sealed system should last the life of the AC — years, not months. If a refill runs out again within a year, the underlying leak wasn't sealed.

**Is R32 gas refill cheaper than R410A?** Marginally — R32 is about ₹200 – ₹500 less per refill than R410A for the same tonnage in Bangalore, because it's more common now and slightly cheaper per kg.

**Do you top up or fully drain-and-recharge?** For a small leak fixed early, a top-up (after vacuum) is fine. If the system's been running low for weeks or the leak was significant, a full recover-vacuum-recharge is the honest job — mixing partial old and new gas plus any moisture that entered is worse than starting clean.

**Can I refill the AC myself with a DIY kit?** No — refrigerant handling requires licensed handling under Indian law, and the wrong pressure or gas type can permanently damage the compressor. This is the one AC job where DIY genuinely doesn't work.
