**Standard split AC installation in Bangalore in 2026 costs ₹1,500 – ₹2,500** for a straightforward job with up to 3 metres of copper piping, wall drilling, standard drainage, indoor-outdoor mounting, vacuum test and a final cooling test. Window AC installation is cheaper (₹800 – ₹1,200); tower AC installation, higher (₹2,500 – ₹4,000). Everything past 3 metres of piping, non-standard stands and stabilisers is extra.

## What's included in a standard install

A "standard" installation quote across every reputable Bangalore installer covers the same core work — if a quote is missing any of these, ask before booking:

- Wall drilling for the indoor unit and copper piping run
- Up to 3 metres of copper piping (both liquid and gas lines, insulated)
- Drainage pipe routing and slope-check
- Mounting the outdoor unit on wall brackets or a floor slab
- Electrical connections between indoor and outdoor units
- Vacuum test on the piping (removes moisture and air before gas release)
- Cooling test with the manufacturer-charged refrigerant

## Where installers commonly upsell

Three things worth knowing before you approve extras:

**Copper piping length.** Standard installs assume the outdoor unit is within 3 metres of the indoor unit. On many Bangalore apartments — especially where the outdoor unit sits in a utility area or on a rear ledge — you'll actually need 5-8 metres. That's ₹1,000 – ₹2,500 in genuine extra work, not overcharging. Ask for the piping route to be measured *before* the drill goes into the wall.

**Stabilisers.** A stabiliser only makes sense if your building genuinely has voltage swings. Modern inverter ACs have built-in wide-range voltage tolerance (usually 145V – 285V). If a technician insists on a stabiliser without checking your inlet voltage, it's an upsell.

**Deep-drilling / core-cutting.** Some walls are old solid concrete and legitimately need a core-cutter, which is a genuine ₹300 – ₹500 extra per hole. But a normal brick wall doesn't — a standard drill handles it.

## What's NOT included (even in good quotes)

- Electrical wiring from your DB (distribution board) to the AC point — that's an electrician's job
- Any woodwork or false-ceiling patching around the piping run
- Society or building-management NOCs for outdoor unit placement
- The stabiliser itself (billed separately if you actually need one)
- Post-install AMC / service contracts

## How long does installation take?

**Split AC** — about 3 to 5 hours from arrival to cooling test, longer if the piping run is complex or a core-cut is needed. **Window AC** — about 90 minutes to 2 hours. **Tower AC** — 4 to 6 hours as it's usually a two-person job.

## FAQs

**Do you install ACs bought online (Amazon, Flipkart, Croma)?** Yes — most brands' warranties are unaffected as long as a certified technician does the install and provides the installation certificate. Keep the installation invoice for warranty claims.

**Do you handle uninstallation only?** Yes — if you're moving, we can uninstall, recover the gas properly, and either handle the reinstall at the new address or hand it over to another installer.

**Do you install second-hand ACs?** Yes, but we recommend a general service first if the unit has been unused for more than 3 months — a lot of second-hand-AC problems trace back to sitting-still moisture, not installation faults.

**Is the visit fee included?** For installations booked online, the visit fee is folded into the installation charge — you don't pay it separately. For repair diagnostic visits, the ₹299 fee is adjusted against the final repair quote.
